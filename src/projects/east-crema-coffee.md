description: |-
  East Crema Coffee is a Spanish chain of coffeeshops founded in 2025. Their ethos is rooted in a passion for specialty coffee, Japanese-inspired design, and the pursuit of authentic, meaningful moments. They work closely with farmers, local businesses, and artisans to create a positive impact while serving coffee of the highest quality. I had the pleasure of collaborating closely with them on their graphic design and motion graphics.
  During my time with them I was asked to diagram a format for a square magazine to be published for their 5 year anniversary, complimented by a motion graphics projects showcasing the history of the opening of each café since their first opening in 2020, and another in collaboration with an illustrator to explain the process the coffee undergoes from bean to cup. Finally I handled the making of mockups for their coffee boxes.
