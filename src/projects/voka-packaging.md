  VOKA Premium Cocktail Glitter
  The goal of the project was to design the packaging for a glitter bottle that could be assembled in-store using no glue, no plastic. 
  Shipping and storing flat packaging is much more space efficient than in ready-made boxes. The concept we came up with allows for the interior of the box to hold the bottle in place for a premium display.
