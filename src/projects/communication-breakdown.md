description: Communication Breakdown is a volunteer-run magazine by musicians, for musicians, based in Madrid. Created as a personal project, it became a platform to amplify the local scene—highlighting bands, venues, studios, photographers, and artists, while fostering a supportive community around independent music. The first issue was printed, and we also produced a podcast to expand the magazine’s reach. As founder, I handled the entire creative vision, editorial design, graphic design, media production, and brand identity.
